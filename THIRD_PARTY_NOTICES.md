# Third-party notices

The renderer directories contain vendored upstream assets. Their license and
notice files are kept next to the corresponding files:

- `renderers/ECharts`: ECharts
- `renderers/Vela`: Vela and third-party notices
- `renderers/VegaLite`: Vega and Vega-Lite
- `renderers/HTML`: Mustache

Rust dependencies retain the licenses declared by their crates. Release
artifacts should include the applicable upstream license texts and this notice
file.
